FONTLOG for Open Runde
======================

This file records the history of Open Runde, a rounded derivative of Inter.

Basic Font Information
----------------------
Open Runde applies optically balanced corner rounding to Inter while retaining
the original family's metrics, glyph coverage, components, kerning, and
OpenType layout behavior.

Version 2.000 - 2026-07-13
--------------------------
- Rebuilt from Inter 4.001 sources at optical size 14.
- Expanded the family to Thin through Black (100-900).
- Added a true italic counterpart for every weight.
- Replaced the previous Glyphs Mini rounding with the reviewed Open Runde v10
  geometry algorithm.
- Added automatic straight-rail reconstruction for eligible long-edge joins.
- Added complete copyright, authorship, license, naming, style-linking, and
  embedding metadata.
- Changed the canonical desktop export to TrueType and regenerated WOFF2 files.
- Added an individual full-character proof for every family member.
- Removed the obsolete four-file Glyphs source directory.

Version 1.001 - 2023
--------------------
- Published Regular, Medium, SemiBold, and Bold upright styles generated with
  the Glyphs Mini rounded-corners tool.
